OperiQ Privacy Policy
Effective Date: November 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how OperiQ collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the OperiQ mobile application and related services (collectively, the "Service").

By using OperiQ, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.
1. Information We Collect
Depending on how you use OperiQ, we may collect the following categories of information.
Account and Profile Information
This may include email address, display name, authentication and account identifiers, company or business name, and account role.
Business and Operational Information
OperiQ may process information you or authorized users enter into the Service, including customer names and contact information, job and service details, job addresses, schedules, notes, assignment information, service requests, and communications associated with jobs or customers.
Photos, Files, and Other Content
If you choose to use features that upload photos, files, or other media, OperiQ may process and store that content to provide the requested feature.
Location Information
Certain OperiQ features may use location-related information to support directions, addresses, maps, job locations, or field-service workflows. Location access depends on the features you use and the permissions you grant through your device.
Subscription and Purchase Information
If you purchase an OperiQ subscription through Apple's App Store, Apple processes the payment.

OperiQ and its subscription-service providers may receive information needed to determine access, such as subscription product, entitlement or plan, subscription status, renewal status, expiration or renewal date, store environment, and app-user or transaction-related identifiers.

OperiQ does not receive your full payment-card number from Apple.
Device, App, and Technical Information
We may process limited technical information needed to operate and secure the Service, such as app version, device or platform information, diagnostic information, error information, authentication/session information, service logs, and timestamps.
2. How We Use Information
We use information to create and maintain accounts; provide OperiQ features and workflows; organize customers, jobs, schedules, team access, and field operations; authenticate users and enforce permissions; verify subscription access; provide support; maintain, troubleshoot, secure, and improve the Service; prevent abuse and unauthorized access; and comply with applicable law.
3. Legal Bases and User Choices
Where applicable law requires a legal basis for processing, we process information as necessary to provide the Service, fulfill contractual obligations, comply with law, protect legitimate interests such as security and service reliability, and where required, based on consent.

You can control certain permissions, such as location, photos, or notifications, through your device settings. Disabling a permission may limit the related OperiQ feature.
4. How We Share Information
We do not sell personal information to advertisers.

We may share information with service providers that help operate OperiQ, including providers for cloud hosting and databases, authentication and infrastructure, subscription and entitlement management, app-store billing, communications, notifications, diagnostics, security, and technical operations.

We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect rights or safety, or in connection with a business transaction such as a merger or acquisition, subject to applicable law.
5. Apple and RevenueCat
OperiQ subscriptions purchased on iOS are processed through Apple's App Store.

OperiQ may use RevenueCat to help manage subscription status, entitlements, paywalls, and related subscription functionality.

Apple and RevenueCat process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.
6. Supabase and Cloud Infrastructure
OperiQ may use Supabase and related cloud infrastructure to provide authentication, database, storage, and backend functionality.

Information submitted to OperiQ may be stored or processed through those systems as necessary to provide the Service.
7. Data Retention
We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Service, maintain your account and business records, fulfill the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, and comply with legal, tax, accounting, security, or dispute-resolution requirements.

When information is no longer reasonably necessary, we may delete, anonymize, or de-identify it, subject to applicable legal and technical requirements.
8. Data Security
We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

No electronic system, storage system, or transmission method is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
9. Your Privacy Rights
Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability, or withdrawal of consent regarding certain personal information.

These rights may be subject to legal exceptions and verification requirements.

To make a privacy request, contact us at the email address below.

Where supported, you may request deletion of your OperiQ account and associated personal information.

Certain information may be retained where reasonably necessary for legal obligations, fraud prevention, security, dispute resolution, or other lawful purposes.

Deleting your OperiQ account does not necessarily cancel an active App Store subscription. App Store subscriptions must be managed through Apple's subscription settings.
11. Children's Privacy
OperiQ is intended for business and field-service use and is not directed to children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.
12. International Processing
Our service providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for cross-border processing in accordance with applicable law.
